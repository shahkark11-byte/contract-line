# The Contract Line

**Scots contract law as a metro map**, built for the New College Lanarkshire Commercial Law poster challenge (2026) by **Shahkar Khan**.

**Live site:** https://shahkark11-byte.github.io/contract-line/

The A2 poster's QR code opens this site. It is made to be used on a phone in the college atrium.

## What's here

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Home** | The three lines: LO1 Formation, LO2 Validity, LO3 Termination |
| **Sam's story** | 11 swipeable cards that follow one first-car deal along all three lines |
| **Lines** | Every key term as a station, with a plain-English explanation, an example, the cases from the course and a link to the official Act |
| **Videos** | Six short vertical films narrated by Shahkar, with captions |
| **Quiz** | "Is it a contract?" 10 scenarios with instant feedback |
| **More** | The poster (view or download), glossary, contracts around campus, sources, about |

## Accuracy

Content follows the New College Lanarkshire Commercial Law LO1–LO3 slides and handouts. Statute links go to legislation.gov.uk.
The Contract (Formation and Remedies) (Scotland) Act 2026 received Royal Assent on 14 April 2026; its main sections, including the end of the postal rule, apply once they are brought into force.

This is a student project, not legal advice. No cookies, no tracking.
